## Open decisions (Graham to answer; the answer and his reason go here, then into `docs/decisions.md`)
1. Flow shape: one short screen per group of questions (Freightquote's Guided layout), or locations and item details together on one page (FreightCenter, Freightera)? Graham's preference so far: separate pages, but he is unsure whether the typical target customer would mind (unconfirmed). Facts that bear on it: Freightquote offers a Quick/Guided layout toggle (only Guided was seen); Graham's stated objection to FreightCenter was the density and amount of typing, and Freightera's single page has far fewer fields; the API takes one request, so the choice is front-end only. Plan: keep this open until sites 5 to 8 have been reviewed (all 8 now seen). Final answer and reason: **DECIDED 2026-11-25 (Graham):** Group A (locations, location types, pickup date, liftgate and appointment) and Group B (item details) go on separate pages. His reason: "they require the user to fill out forms whose data is not directly related". Graham's earlier doubt about whether the typical target customer would mind is not settled by this; it is his preference.
2. Freight class: show the calculated class as a suggestion the shipper can edit (Freightquote), a plain dropdown (FreightCenter), or hide it unless the shipper changes it? Answer and reason: **DECIDED 2026-11-25 (Graham):** an editable suggestion: show the class calculated from density and let the shipper change it. His reason: "This will help avoid mistakes by customers who are confused about the class system".
3. Location types: show the four types (business dock, residential, limited access, construction site) as cards with a one-line explanation, as Freightquote does, or as plain choices? Answer and reason: **DECIDED 2026-11-25 (Graham):** radio buttons. His reason: "this allows the customer to easily see all the options and make choices the way they would if they were checking boxes on a physical form". Not stated by Graham: whether each radio option carries a one-line explanation.

Answer and reason: **DECIDED 2026-11-25 (Graham):** "I want them next to each other with a \"?\" widget pop up with an explanation".
